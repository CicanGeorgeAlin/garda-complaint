Garda complaint — in plain English

Under Part 6 of the Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024, a complaint may be made by a relevant member of the public who was directly affected by, or witnessed, the act or omission complained of. A complaint may also be made on another person's behalf in the circumstances set out in section 195.

No Irish citizenship requirement stated in s.195 Witnesses can complain Representatives can complain in defined cases Normally 12 months
