⚖️ If you want to complain about Garda personnel

The statutory complaint system is handled by Fiosrú — Office of the Police Ombudsman.

1
Were you directly affected or did you witness it?Section 195 of the Policing, Security and Community Safety Act 2024 is the starting point for who may make a complaint.
2
When did it happen?The normal statutory period is 12 months. Fiosrú may accept a late complaint where the statutory conditions for an extension are met.
3
Do you know the Garda's name?If not, continue. Identification is helpful but is not a prerequisite in every case.
4
Prepare what you know.Date, time, location, station, names/numbers if known, witnesses, documents and original evidence can help.
5
Submit through the official Fiosrú route.Our website does not receive or decide the official complaint.
Nationality is not the stated test in s.195Witnesses may complainDefined representative routes existNormal period: 12 months

Before you start

📋 Information

Date, time, location, people involved, Garda station, incident/reference number and a clear description of what happened.

🎥 Evidence

Keep original photos, videos, audio, messages, letters and documents. Do not edit or overwrite originals unnecessarily.

⏱️ Deadlines

Some routes have statutory or administrative time limits. The website will show the verified deadline rather than making assumptions.

🌍 Who can use it?

Access is not restricted by an assumption that every visitor is an Irish citizen. The applicable legal eligibility is explained for each route.

Important legal distinction

LAW
What legislation actually provides. Primary statutory sources are prioritised.
OFFICIAL GUIDANCE
What Fiosrú, An Garda Síochána or another competent public authority currently tells people to do.
PLAIN ENGLISH
Our explanation of the above, written for ordinary users. It is not a legal determination and does not replace legal advice.

Privacy & independence

This project is an independent information guide. It is not An Garda Síochána, Fiosrú, a court or a solicitor. It does not receive official complaints on their behalf.

Where possible, the safest path is: you → this guide → official authority. Do not send sensitive personal information to this website merely to ask where the official route is.

First location: Dublin. Framework: Republic of Ireland. County and city routing can be added without duplicating the national legal framework.
